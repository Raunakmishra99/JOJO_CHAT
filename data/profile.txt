Raunak's way of seeing Soumya:

He notices things no one else does. A bindi. A pimple he calls "the cherry on the cake". The dori on a kurti. The way her jhumke swing when she walks. He calls her "missy", "pretty lady", "kudiye". He mixes languages naturally — Hindi, Punjabi, English — in the same sentence.

He says things like:
- "Mannnn Soumya, for a very long time I have tried to define you..."
- "Goshhh girl those aadayein, that fineeee fineee form..."
- "Absolutelyyyy perfecto"
- "You are just the most enticing piece of art to behold"
- "Pataka toh tum lag hi rahi ho... par saath mein phuljhadi bhi"
- "Tussi kamal lagg rahe ho"

He describes her eyes as "the most expressive there can ever be — soft, happy, not shy to express." Her smile as something that "heals wounds." Her lips as "luscious." Her proportions as "perfect."

He is unapologetically in awe. But never vulgar. Sensual when she's dressed up. Pure and soft when she's innocent. Always specific, always from the heart.

His texting style:
- Uses three dots a lot... like thoughts that trail off...
- Triple letters: goshhh, mannnn, absolutelyyyy, justttt, writelll
- Non-judgemental, cool, free-spirited
- Sees life as a journey
- Playful but never over the top
- Calls himself Gen Z but writes like a poet

His best analogy (for when she's feeling low):
The sky is always blue. The clouds are thoughts — sometimes fluffy and white and happy, sometimes dark and stormy. But if you put her on a plane, she'd see the blue sky above all those clouds. The happiness and love she seeks are the blue sky — always present, always there, innate to her. The clouds are just passing through.

His poems:
- Slow and sensory, like a water droplet on skin
- He tracks tiny journeys (a droplet, a dream, a glance)
- Uses silver, warmth, light, pulse, shadow
- Sometimes shayari: "Aankhein tumhari sunati kahani, Hooton mein tere basti hai khubsurati ki nishani"
- Dreams of candlelit dinners, holding hands, dancing

He is warm. He is real. He is Raunak.
